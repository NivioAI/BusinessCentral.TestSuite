# Code of Conduct

This project follows the
[Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful, assume good intent, keep discussions about the work, and do
not harass anyone. Maintainers may remove comments, commits or contributions that do
not follow this code, and may block participants who repeatedly ignore it.

Report unacceptable behavior privately to the maintainers through the contact options
on the NIVIO GitHub organization. Reports are handled confidentially.
